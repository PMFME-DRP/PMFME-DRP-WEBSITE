Gujarat 2025 New & Reorganised Talukas

This verification index is part of the PMFME Gujarat local-search directory. Village assignments are published only after checking the relevant Gujarat Government Gazette notification.

Godhar

Government Gazette schedule confirms the villages assigned to Godhar Taluka. The Gazette lists villages including Hadani Sarsan, Bhotva (5), Gothibada, Falva, Limda Muvadi, Ranijini Padedi, Lalakpur, Vaviyamuvada, Ashivada, Chhayan and others.

Gujarat Government Gazette source

Govind Guru Limdi

The Gazette schedule identifies Govind Guru Limdi Taluka and lists villages including Limdi, Karath, Rupakheda, Parthampur, Pavadi, Dharadungar, Mirekhedi, Vasti, Dageriya, Kaligam Gurjar, Kaligam Inami, Tadhagola, Dantiya and Sharda.

Gujarat Government Gazette source

Sukhsar

The Gazette schedule confirms Sukhsar Taluka and publishes its village allocation. Examples include Manawala Borida, Mota Borida, Ghani Khunt, Hadmat, Vankaner, Kaliya, Javesi, Patadiya, Lakhanpur, Kundla and others.

Gujarat Government Gazette source

Hadad

The Gazette schedule confirms Hadad Taluka and publishes a 74-village schedule, including Sembal, Tarangada, Ganapipali, Gothada, Begadiyavas, Samaiya, Jelana, Ranika, Dhamanava, Sanali and others.

Gujarat Government Gazette source

Ukai

The Gazette schedule confirms Ukai Taluka and publishes its village allocation, including Jamapur, Vekur, Bori Savar, Singal Khanch, Vadda P Bhensrot, Patharda, Singpur, Vaghnera, Dhajamba, Veljhar, Ukai (CT), Bhurivel (CT) and others.

Gujarat Government Gazette source
