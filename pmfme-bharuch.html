ODOP: Banana based Products

Bharuch PMFME ODOP focus

The current Gujarat ODOP list identifies Banana based Products for Bharuch district. This provides a relevant starting point for entrepreneurs evaluating banana value-addition projects, subject to current scheme requirements and commercial feasibility.

Banana-processing opportunities to evaluate

  • Banana chips and snack products.
  • Banana flour or powder for suitable food applications.
  • Dehydrated banana and shelf-stable packaged products.
  • Pulp and other value-added banana products where technology and market demand are suitable.

A viable project should consider raw-material sourcing, seasonality, processing yield, machinery, packaging, food safety, working capital and market access.

Local project planning

Bharuch has a mix of agricultural, commercial and industrial activity. Entrepreneurs should select a project location and processing model based on raw-material access, utilities, labour, logistics and the intended market. The district page should be treated as planning guidance rather than a guarantee of scheme approval.

PMFME project checklist

  1. Define product and production capacity.
  2. Estimate machinery, premises, utilities and working capital.
  3. Check PMFME eligibility and admissible costs under current guidelines.
  4. Prepare a bankable project plan and current quotations where required.
