ODOP: Banana based Products

Anand PMFME ODOP focus

The revised Gujarat ODOP list identifies Banana based Products for Anand district. Banana value addition can be evaluated as a starting point for a micro food-processing project, subject to current PMFME conditions and commercial feasibility.

Banana-processing ideas to evaluate

  • Banana chips and snack products.
  • Banana flour or powder for food applications.
  • Dehydrated banana products with suitable packaging.
  • Banana pulp and other value-added formats where technology and market demand support the model.

Evaluate seasonality, sourcing, product shelf life, machinery, packaging and target market before selecting the final project.

PMFME project checklist for Anand

  1. Define product, capacity and customer segment.
  2. Estimate machinery, premises, working capital and project costs.
  3. Check current PMFME eligibility and admissible costs.
  4. Prepare a practical project report and current quotations where required.
  5. Discuss the proposed finance structure with the relevant bank.
