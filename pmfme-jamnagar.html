ODOP: Groundnut based Products

Jamnagar PMFME ODOP focus

The current Gujarat ODOP list identifies Groundnut based Products for Jamnagar district. Groundnut value addition can therefore be considered as a priority direction for suitable PMFME project concepts, subject to current guidelines.

Groundnut-processing ideas to evaluate

  • Groundnut cleaning, grading and hygienic retail packing.
  • Roasted and flavoured groundnut snacks.
  • Groundnut oil and related value-added products where technically feasible.
  • Peanut-based snack, confectionery or ingredient formats with a clear market.

Project selection should be based on raw-material sourcing, product economics, machinery, food safety, packaging, working capital and market demand.

PMFME project preparation for Jamnagar

  1. Choose a specific product and processing capacity.
  2. Estimate machinery, premises, utilities and working capital.
  3. Check eligibility and admissible costs under current PMFME rules.
  4. Prepare a practical project report and current supplier quotations.
  5. Discuss the finance structure with the relevant bank before submission.
