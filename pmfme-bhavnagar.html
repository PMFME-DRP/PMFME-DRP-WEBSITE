ODOP: Onion based Products

Bhavnagar PMFME ODOP focus

The revised Ministry of Food Processing Industries Gujarat ODOP list identifies Onion based Products for Bhavnagar district. Entrepreneurs can use this ODOP focus as a starting point for evaluating value addition, subject to current PMFME rules and project feasibility.

Onion processing ideas to evaluate

  • Dehydrated onion and onion flakes.
  • Onion powder and food-service ingredients.
  • Fried or crispy onion products.
  • Cleaned, graded and packaged onions.
  • Other shelf-stable onion value-added products where technically feasible.

Assess raw-material seasonality, drying technology, hygiene, packaging, shelf life, market demand, working capital and energy requirements before selecting the project.

PMFME project planning for Bhavnagar

A practical project should clearly define product, processing flow, capacity, machinery, premises, utilities, working capital and market. Eligibility and admissible costs should be checked against the current PMFME guidelines before submission.

Project checklist

  1. Select the onion product and target customer.
  2. Plan sourcing, cleaning, cutting, drying/processing and packaging as applicable.
  3. Estimate machinery and project costs using current quotations.
  4. Check PMFME eligibility and admissible costs.
  5. Prepare a bankable project report and finance plan.
