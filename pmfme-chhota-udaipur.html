Maize processing ideas to evaluate

  • Maize flour and meal.
  • Grits and snack inputs.
  • Ready-to-cook maize mixes.

Choose a product only after checking raw-material supply, machinery, food safety, market demand and financial feasibility.

PMFME project checklist

  1. Define product, capacity and target market.
  2. Estimate machinery, premises and working capital.
  3. Check current eligibility and admissible costs.
  4. Prepare the project report and required quotations.
