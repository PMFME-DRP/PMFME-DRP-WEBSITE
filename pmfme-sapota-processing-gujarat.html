Product concepts to evaluate

Depending on technical and market feasibility, entrepreneurs can evaluate pulp, beverages, dried sapota products and other shelf-stable value-added foods.

Build a bankable project

  • Study seasonal raw-material availability.
  • Select a specific product and capacity.
  • Plan washing, pulping, drying, processing and packaging equipment as applicable.
  • Use current supplier quotations.
  • Prepare realistic sales, operating-cost and working-capital assumptions.
  • Check current eligible-cost rules before calculating subsidy.
