PMFME Guidance for Tapi

Sorghum-focused food-processing guidance for micro-enterprise project planning.

Tapi ODOP focus

The revised Gujarat ODOP list identifies Sorghum based Products for Tapi district. Sorghum can be evaluated for flour, mixes, snacks and other value-added food products where sourcing and market demand support the project.

Sorghum-processing ideas

  • Sorghum flour and cleaned grain products.
  • Multigrain flour and ready-to-cook mixes.
  • Traditional or modern sorghum snacks.
  • Packaged value-added sorghum foods.

Consider product formulation, milling technology, packaging, food safety, shelf life and target customers.

Project checklist

  1. Define product, capacity and market.
  2. Estimate machinery, premises and working capital.
  3. Check current PMFME eligibility and admissible costs.
  4. Prepare project and bank documents with current quotations.
