FOOD BUSINESS OPPORTUNITIES

What Kind of Food Businesses Can Be Explored?

Food processing covers a wide range of activities. Eligibility depends on the applicable PMFME guidelines and the nature of the proposed unit.

🌾 Grain & Flour Processing

Flour mills, grain processing, millet processing, cleaning, grading and related food processing activities.

🥭 Fruits & Vegetables

Dehydration, processing, preservation, pulping, packaging and value-added food products.

🥫 Spices & Food Products

Spice processing, blended products, packaged food products and other eligible processing activities.

🥛 Dairy & Related Processing

Explore eligible dairy and food processing activities according to applicable guidelines.

🍪 Bakery & Snacks

Bakery products, snacks and other food processing enterprises may be explored subject to eligibility.

📦 Value Added Food Products

Convert agricultural produce into value-added food products with suitable processing and packaging.

WHO CAN APPLY?

Check Your Basic Eligibility

Eligibility should always be checked against the applicable PMFME guidelines and the specific facts of the enterprise.

Potential Applicant Profile

  • Existing eligible food processing entrepreneurs
  • Individual food processing units meeting scheme requirements
  • Entrepreneurs planning eligible food processing activities
  • Businesses seeking formalisation and capacity enhancement
  • Applicants able to meet required contribution and financing conditions

Before You Apply

  • Identify the exact food processing activity
  • Estimate machinery and project requirements
  • Prepare a realistic project cost
  • Keep required documents ready
  • Discuss financing requirements with the concerned bank

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Some common questions about PMFME and food processing projects.

What is PMFME?

PMFME is the Prime Minister Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises scheme, aimed at supporting eligible micro food processing enterprises through applicable scheme interventions.

What subsidy is available?

Under the individual unit component, the scheme provides credit-linked capital subsidy of 35% of eligible project cost, subject to the applicable ceiling and scheme guidelines.

Is every food business eligible?

No. Eligibility depends on the nature of the food processing activity, applicant profile, project and applicable PMFME guidelines.

Can I get help in preparing my project?

You can contact PMFME-DRP for practical guidance regarding project planning, documents, machinery and the application process. Final eligibility and approval remain subject to the concerned authorities and financing institution.

Is this the official PMFME government website?

No. This website is an independent informational and guidance resource created for PMFME-related awareness and assistance. It is not an official Government of India portal.

How can I contact PMFME-DRP?

You can directly contact PMFME-DRP on WhatsApp at +91 70410 75170.
