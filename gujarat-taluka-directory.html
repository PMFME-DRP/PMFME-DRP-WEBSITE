Current administrative reference

The Directorate of Economics & Statistics currently publishes a Gujarat taluka list, while Gujarat's 2025 administrative reorganisation introduced Vav-Tharad district and 17 additional talukas. Because village allocations and administrative notifications can change, this page separates the published taluka reference from newly notified/reorganised units instead of silently mixing old and new datasets.

Informational resource only. This website is not an official Government of India or Government of Gujarat portal. Verify current administrative boundaries with the latest government notification before relying on them for legal, land, revenue or application purposes.
