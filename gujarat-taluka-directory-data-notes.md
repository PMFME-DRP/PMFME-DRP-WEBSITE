# Gujarat taluka directory — data notes

## Source hierarchy

1. Gujarat Directorate of Economics & Statistics — published List of Talukas of Gujarat.
2. Gujarat Panchayat Department — district/taluka/Gram Panchayat lists.
3. Gujarat Revenue Department notifications — boundary and village reallocations.
4. District administration sites for local verification.

## Important implementation rule

The public directory is a discovery/SEO layer, not a legal administrative register. Before publishing a dedicated taluka or village landing page, reconcile the locality against the latest government notification. Do not infer current boundaries from historical Census or old third-party lists.

## 2025 reorganisation

The 2025 cabinet reorganisation introduced Vav-Tharad district and 17 new/reorganised talukas: Godhar, Kothamba, Chikda, Nanapodha, Rah, Dharnidhar, Ogad, Hadad, Guru Govind Limbdi, Sukhsar, Kadval, Fagvel, Shamlaji, Sathamba, Ukai, Areth and Ambika.

These should remain clearly marked as new/reorganised until their village schedules are reconciled from the relevant Revenue Department notifications.

## Why the directory is intentionally conservative

A directory can be useful for searches such as “PMFME in [taluka]” only when the page contains meaningful local information. Generating thousands of near-identical locality pages would create low-value pages and can harm rather than help search visibility.

The implementation therefore separates:

- verified administrative directory data;
- local PMFME opportunity content;
- future village/area pages that require source verification.
