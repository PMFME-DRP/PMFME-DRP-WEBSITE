Value-added product ideas

Potential concepts to evaluate include pulp, juice or beverage bases, dried mango products, pickle and other shelf-stable value-added foods, depending on technical feasibility, food-safety requirements and market demand.

Build the project around evidence

  • Map mango availability and seasonality.
  • Choose a product and realistic production capacity.
  • Identify washing, pulping, drying, cooking or packaging equipment as applicable.
  • Use current supplier quotations.
  • Prepare realistic sales, cost and working-capital projections.
  • Check the current PMFME eligible-cost rules before calculating subsidy.

PMFME and ODOP

MoFPI states that ODOP provides a framework for value-chain development and gives preference to ODOP production in relevant support components, while final support remains subject to scheme conditions. citeturn0search0
