Gujarat PMFME District → Taluka → Area Directory

This page documents the data-first rollout of the Gujarat local PMFME directory. Administrative names and village allocations are verified before an indexable page is published.

Current administrative reference

The Gujarat Socio-Economic Review 2025-26 reports 34 districts and 267 talukas as the administrative setup as of 31 December 2025, while it separately reports 265 Taluka Panchayats. This distinction is retained in our data rather than collapsing the two figures into one claim.

2025 new-taluka layer

Seventeen new talukas were created during the 2025 administrative restructuring. Their source talukas and districts are stored in data/gujarat-local-seo-data.json.

Publishing rules

  • District pages: unique PMFME/ODOP/local food-processing content.
  • Taluka pages: publish only after current administrative mapping is verified.
  • Village/area pages: create only when there is useful unique local information.
  • Do not mass-publish near-identical doorway pages.
  • Every published local page links to PMFME eligibility, subsidy, business ideas and application guidance.

Source hierarchy

  1. Gujarat Government Gazette / Revenue Department for boundary and village allocation changes.
  2. Gujarat Directorate of Economics & Statistics for administrative reference lists.
  3. District administration pages for current local directories.
  4. Other sources only as secondary cross-checks.
