PMFME Eligibility in Gujarat

Check the basic eligibility factors before preparing a food-processing project under PMFME.

Basic eligibility points

  • For individual support, PMFME guidance identifies micro food processing enterprises and focuses on upgrading eligible units.
  • The official FAQ states an applicant should be above 18 years of age and have at least VIII-standard educational qualification.
  • The applicant should have ownership rights in the enterprise; proprietorship and partnership structures are addressed in the official FAQ.
  • For individual capital investment support, preference is given to units producing the district's identified ODOP product. Existing units producing other products may also be supported subject to the applicable scheme conditions.
  • New individual or group units are subject to ODOP-related conditions under the scheme guidelines.

What to prepare

Keep the product, proposed location, existing/new-unit status, machinery requirement, estimated project cost, applicant details and financing plan ready for discussion. Eligibility is not determined by this website alone.

Application pathway

The official PMFME FAQ describes the individual pathway as Registration → Submission → DRP → DLC → Bank. Actual processing can depend on the applicable workflow and authorities.
