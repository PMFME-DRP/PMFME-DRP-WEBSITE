ODOP: Groundnut based Products

Rajkot PMFME ODOP focus

The revised Ministry of Food Processing Industries Gujarat ODOP list identifies Groundnut based Products for Rajkot district. This creates a useful starting point for evaluating value-added groundnut businesses, subject to current PMFME conditions, technical feasibility and market demand.

Groundnut processing ideas to evaluate

  • Cleaned, graded and packaged groundnuts.
  • Roasted and flavoured groundnut snacks.
  • Groundnut powder, flour and ingredient products.
  • Peanut/groundnut butter and other value-added products.
  • Groundnut oil or related processing where the project is technically and financially viable.

Project selection should consider raw-material sourcing, quality control, machinery, capacity, packaging, food-safety requirements, working capital and market access.

Rajkot local project planning

Rajkot district has a strong agricultural and enterprise context. A project should be designed around the actual location, available raw material, utilities, premises, workforce, machinery and target market rather than selecting equipment first.

PMFME project checklist for Rajkot

  1. Define the groundnut product and target market.
  2. Confirm raw-material availability and quality requirements.
  3. Estimate machinery, premises, utilities and working capital.
  4. Check current PMFME eligibility and admissible project costs.
  5. Prepare a bankable project report and current quotations where required.
