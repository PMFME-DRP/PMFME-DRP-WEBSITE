PMFME Guidance for Navsari

Food-processing project guidance with a focus on Navsari's Mango-based ODOP opportunity.

Navsari ODOP focus

The Ministry of Food Processing Industries' revised Gujarat ODOP list identifies Mango based Products for Navsari district.

Business ideas to evaluate

Entrepreneurs can evaluate mango pulp, juice or beverages, dehydrated mango, pickles, pulp-based products and other value-added mango processing concepts where raw-material supply and market demand support the project.

Before preparing a project

Check seasonality, raw-material sourcing, machinery, cold-chain or storage needs, working capital, market demand and applicable PMFME conditions. Eligibility and admissible costs must be confirmed under current guidelines.
