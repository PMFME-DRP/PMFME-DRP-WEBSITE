Processing ideas to evaluate

Depending on market demand and technical feasibility, entrepreneurs can evaluate roasted groundnut products, groundnut-based snacks, peanut butter, groundnut flour and packaged value-added products. These are business ideas, not automatic PMFME approvals.

Project planning

  1. Define product, pack size and target customer.
  2. Confirm raw-material sourcing and quality requirements.
  3. Choose cleaning, grading, roasting, grinding and packaging equipment as applicable.
  4. Collect current quotations and build a realistic project cost.
  5. Prepare capacity, sales, expenses, working capital and repayment assumptions.
  6. Check eligible cost and current PMFME conditions before calculating subsidy.

Connect the project to PMFME
