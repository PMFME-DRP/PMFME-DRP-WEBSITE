PMFME Guidance for Kutch

Date-palm focused food-processing and micro-enterprise project guidance.

Kutch ODOP focus

The revised Gujarat ODOP list identifies Datepalm based products for Kutch district. Date-palm value addition offers a distinct product direction for evaluating processing and packaging projects.

Date-palm processing ideas

  • Cleaned and packaged dates.
  • Date paste and date syrup.
  • Dried and value-added date products.
  • Date-based snack or ingredient products where formulation and market demand support the concept.

Check raw-material quality, processing technology, shelf life, packaging and market positioning.

Project checklist

  1. Define product, capacity and target market.
  2. Plan machinery, premises, storage and working capital.
  3. Check current PMFME eligibility and admissible costs.
  4. Prepare project and bank documents with current quotations.
