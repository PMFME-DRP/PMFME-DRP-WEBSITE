What is ODOP under PMFME?

One District One Product (ODOP) is a core part of the PMFME approach. Official PMFME material explains that the ODOP approach is intended to help develop value chains, common services and market opportunities around identified products. Conditions differ by component, so the current official list and guidelines should always be checked before a project is finalised.

Why ODOP matters for a Gujarat food-processing project

The district product can influence the type of opportunity that is most relevant to explore, especially for new units and components where ODOP is a condition. A good project should still be tested for raw-material availability, technology, market demand, food safety, capacity and financial viability.
