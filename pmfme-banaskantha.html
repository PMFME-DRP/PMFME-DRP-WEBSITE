PMFME Guidance for Banaskantha

Pomegranate-focused food-processing guidance for district entrepreneurs.

Banaskantha ODOP focus

The revised Gujarat ODOP list identifies Pomegranate based products for Banaskantha district. This creates a focused starting point for evaluating fruit value-addition projects under applicable PMFME conditions.

Pomegranate-processing ideas

  • Fresh pomegranate aril processing and packaging.
  • Pomegranate juice and beverage products.
  • Concentrates and other pulp-based formats.
  • Dehydrated or shelf-stable value-added products where technically feasible.

Consider fruit quality, seasonality, processing technology, storage, packaging and market demand before selecting the project.

Project preparation

  1. Define product and target market.
  2. Estimate machinery, premises, storage and working capital.
  3. Check current PMFME eligibility and admissible costs.
  4. Prepare a practical project report and current quotations.
