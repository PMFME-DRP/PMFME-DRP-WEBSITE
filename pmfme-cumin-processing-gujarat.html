Processing concepts

Entrepreneurs can evaluate cleaning, grading, grinding, blending and consumer-packaging concepts for cumin and cumin-based food products, depending on the intended market and technical setup.

Project-report checklist

  1. Define product and target market.
  2. Confirm raw-material quality and supply.
  3. Select cleaning, grading, milling and packaging equipment as required.
  4. Collect current quotations.
  5. Prepare capacity, sales, expenses and working-capital projections.
  6. Verify eligible cost and applicable PMFME conditions before calculating subsidy.
