FAQ

Common PMFME questions

What is PMFME?

PMFME is the Prime Minister Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises scheme supporting eligible micro food processing enterprises through applicable scheme interventions.

What subsidy is available?

For eligible individual units, the current information on this page describes a credit-linked capital subsidy of 35% of eligible project cost, subject to the applicable ceiling and scheme guidelines.

Can every food business get PMFME?

No. Eligibility depends on the food-processing activity, applicant profile, project and applicable PMFME guidelines.

Can DRP Shankar Pradhan help me plan my project?

You can share your product, location, project cost and requirements through the guidance form. DRP Shankar Pradhan can then discuss the appropriate next steps.

Is this the official Government PMFME website?

No. This is an independent informational and guidance website. It is not an official Government of India portal.
