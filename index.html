WHY EXPLORE PMFME?

Support for Your Food Business Journey

Whether you are planning a new food processing unit or strengthening an existing enterprise, proper guidance can help you understand the scheme better.

💰
35%

Credit-Linked Subsidy

For eligible individual micro food processing units, PMFME provides credit-linked capital subsidy at 35% of eligible project cost, subject to a maximum ceiling of ₹10 lakh per unit.
