Turn an ODOP idea into a project

  1. Confirm the current district ODOP and applicable eligibility.
  2. Choose a realistic processed product and capacity.
  3. Obtain current machinery quotations and prepare project costing.
  4. Build a practical DPR with market and financial assumptions.
  5. Review the applicable PMFME subsidy and bank-finance process.
