PMFME Guidance for Botad

District-specific guidance for food businesses exploring the Guava based Products ODOP opportunity.

Botad PMFME ODOP focus

The approved Gujarat ODOP list identifies Guava based Products for Botad district. Guava value addition can be evaluated for a PMFME project where sourcing, technology and market demand support the concept.

Guava processing ideas

  • Guava pulp and juice.
  • Jelly, jam and fruit preparations.
  • Dehydrated guava and powder.
  • Other packaged value-added guava products.

Project preparation

Assess seasonal sourcing, processing equipment, premises, storage, packaging, working capital, food-safety requirements and market demand. Confirm current PMFME eligibility and admissible costs before applying.
