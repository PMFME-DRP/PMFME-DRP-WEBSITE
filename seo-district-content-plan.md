# PMFME Gujarat District SEO Content Plan

## Strategy
Build Gujarat-wide topical authority around PMFME, then connect each district to its official ODOP product, practical processing opportunities, scheme guidance, and relevant local pages.

## Priority districts
Ahmedabad, Rajkot, Bhavnagar, Vadodara, Surat, Bharuch, Jamnagar, Junagadh, Anand, Mehsana, Kutch, Navsari, Valsad, Banaskantha, Amreli.

## Required content per district
- PMFME + district search intent in title/H1 and opening copy
- Official ODOP product and a clear statement that eligibility remains subject to current guidelines
- 3–6 genuinely relevant processing/business opportunities
- PMFME eligibility/subsidy/application links
- Related district/taluka pages only where useful
- Unique FAQ based on actual district intent; no keyword stuffing
- Self-referencing canonical URL
- WebPage/BreadcrumbList structured data where valid
- Link back to Gujarat district directory and PMFME Gujarat hub

## Internal-linking model
PMFME Gujarat → District Directory → District page → ODOP/opportunity content → Eligibility/Subsidy/Application resources → relevant local page.

## Quality rule
Do not create pages solely by swapping district names. If a district page cannot provide useful, district-specific information, consolidate or defer it rather than publishing thin content.

## Indexing rule
Keep sitemap entries limited to canonical, live, useful URLs. After deployment, submit the sitemap and selectively inspect important URLs in Google Search Console. Do not claim indexing or ranking until Search Console confirms it.
