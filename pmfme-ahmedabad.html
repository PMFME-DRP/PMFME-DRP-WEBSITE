ODOP: Wheat-based Products

Ahmedabad PMFME ODOP focus

The revised Gujarat ODOP list identifies Wheat based Products for Ahmedabad district. This makes wheat value addition a natural starting point for evaluating a PMFME project, subject to current scheme conditions and project feasibility.

Food-processing ideas to evaluate

  • Flour and atta processing with improved packaging and branding.
  • Multigrain or fortified flour mixes where technically and commercially feasible.
  • Ready-to-cook wheat products and premixes.
  • Bakery inputs and other value-added wheat products.

The right project depends on raw-material access, machinery, capacity, market demand, food-safety requirements and financial viability.

PMFME project checklist for Ahmedabad

  1. Define the product and target market.
  2. Estimate machinery, premises, working capital and other project costs.
  3. Check eligibility and admissible costs under the current PMFME guidelines.
  4. Prepare a practical project report and current quotations where required.
  5. Discuss the finance structure with the relevant bank before final submission.
