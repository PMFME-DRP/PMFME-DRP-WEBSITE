How to use this directory for PMFME planning

  • Identify the taluka and local market where the proposed food-processing unit will operate.
  • Check the relevant district ODOP product and current PMFME conditions before finalising the product.
  • Use local raw-material availability, seasonality, transport and buyer access when preparing the project.
  • For village-level coverage, use the official Surat revenue-village directory rather than treating this page as a substitute for government records.
