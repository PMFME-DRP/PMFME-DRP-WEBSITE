# Gujarat Locality SEO Publishing Rules

## Hierarchy
Gujarat → District → Taluka → Village/Area → PMFME opportunity.

## Data rule
Use the latest Government of Gujarat administrative source for district/taluka structure and the relevant Gujarat Government Gazette for village allocation when boundaries have changed.

## Page rule
A locality page must contain useful local information. Do not generate thousands of near-identical pages containing only a place name and generic PMFME text.

## Minimum useful content
- verified district and taluka relationship
- verified village/area name where applicable
- relevant local food/agri-processing context
- PMFME guidance that is actually applicable
- internal links to eligibility, subsidy, business ideas and application guidance
- source/disclaimer where administrative data may change

## Current verification batch
The 2025 Gazette schedules have been verified for Godhar, Govind Guru Limdi, Sukhsar, Hadad and Ukai. See `new-taluka-verification-index.html`.

## SEO principle
Index quality pages, not every possible keyword permutation. Directory entries may remain non-indexed until sufficient useful content exists.
