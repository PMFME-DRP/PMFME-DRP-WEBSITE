How the journey works

A simple roadmap from business idea to project implementation.

1

Discuss

Share your product, business status, location and approximate project requirement.

2

Check Eligibility

Review the applicant, product, unit and applicable PMFME component.

3

Prepare Project

Structure machinery, project cost, working capital and business information.

4

Bank Process

Proceed through the applicable bank-financing and sanction process.

5

Subsidy Process

Eligible subsidy is processed according to scheme rules and bank procedure.

6

Grow

Implement the approved project and strengthen your food-processing enterprise.
