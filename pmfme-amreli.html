District-specific guidance for groundnut-based food processing and micro-enterprise project planning.

ODOP: Groundnut based Products

Amreli PMFME ODOP focus

The revised Gujarat ODOP list identifies Groundnut based Products for Amreli district. Groundnut value addition can support several small-scale processing concepts, subject to raw-material access, market demand and current PMFME conditions.

Groundnut-processing ideas to evaluate

  • Groundnut oil and oilseed processing.
  • Roasted, flavoured and packaged groundnut products.
  • Peanut-based snacks and confectionery products.
  • Groundnut chikki and other value-added formats.

Product selection should consider machinery, food safety, packaging, shelf life, pricing and the intended market.

PMFME project checklist for Amreli

  1. Define product and expected production capacity.
  2. Map raw-material sourcing and storage needs.
  3. Estimate machinery, premises, working capital and project costs.
  4. Check current PMFME eligibility and admissible costs.
  5. Prepare project and bank documents with current quotations where required.
