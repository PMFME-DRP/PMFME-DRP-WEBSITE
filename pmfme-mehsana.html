PMFME Guidance for Mehsana

Cumin-focused food-processing guidance for micro-enterprise project planning.

Mehsana ODOP focus

The revised Gujarat ODOP list identifies Cumin based Products for Mehsana district. This provides a focused direction for spice cleaning, processing, blending and packaging projects.

Cumin-processing ideas

  • Cleaned and graded cumin.
  • Cumin powder and packaged spice products.
  • Blended spice products using suitable formulations.
  • Retail-ready branded cumin products.

Evaluate raw-material quality, cleaning and grinding technology, food safety, packaging and market demand.

Project preparation

  1. Define product and production capacity.
  2. Plan machinery, premises and working capital.
  3. Check current PMFME eligibility and admissible costs.
  4. Prepare a practical project report and current quotations.
