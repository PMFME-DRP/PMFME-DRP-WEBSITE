Gujarat-wide PMFME coverage

The Ministry of Food Processing Industries has approved a PMFME One District One Product (ODOP) for all 33 districts of Gujarat in its current Gujarat ODOP list. The district pages below are built around that official ODOP information and practical food-processing project guidance.

Gujarat's administrative landscape can change, so new or reorganized districts should be added to this site only after the applicable PMFME/ODOP position is verified.
