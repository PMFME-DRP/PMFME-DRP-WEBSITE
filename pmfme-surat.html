PMFME Guidance for Surat

Food-processing project guidance with a focus on Surat's Banana-based ODOP opportunity.

Surat ODOP focus

The Ministry of Food Processing Industries' revised Gujarat ODOP list identifies Banana based Products for Surat district.

Business ideas to evaluate

Depending on raw-material supply, market demand and project feasibility, entrepreneurs can evaluate banana chips and snacks, banana flour or powder, dehydrated products, pulp or other value-added banana products.

Before preparing a project

Check the product, raw-material availability, machinery, premises, working capital, market, project cost and applicable PMFME conditions. Eligibility and admissible costs must be confirmed under current guidelines.
