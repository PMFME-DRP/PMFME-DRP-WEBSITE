PMFME Gujarat FAQ

Common questions about eligibility, subsidy, ODOP and food-processing project preparation.

What is PMFME?

PMFME is the Prime Minister Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises scheme. This website provides independent guidance for understanding the scheme and preparing a food-processing project.

Who can be eligible for individual PMFME support?

Eligibility depends on the applicable PMFME guidelines and applicant and enterprise conditions. See our PMFME eligibility guide for Gujarat.

How much PMFME subsidy is available?

For eligible individual micro food processing units, the credit-linked capital subsidy framework provides 35% of eligible project cost, subject to a maximum of ₹10 lakh per unit. For the individual-unit component, the beneficiary contribution is a minimum of 10%. See the PMFME subsidy guide for eligible-cost details and examples.

What costs can be included in the eligible project cost?

Under the PMFME guideline modification dated 18 May 2022, eligible project cost for individual micro food processing units comprises plant and machinery plus technical civil work. Land cost and rental or lease of a work shed are excluded, and technical civil work should not exceed 30% of the eligible project cost. Final assessment remains subject to the applicable guidelines and bank appraisal.

What is ODOP in PMFME?

ODOP means One District One Product. PMFME uses an ODOP approach, and applicable conditions and priorities depend on the component, district and proposed enterprise.

What food processing business ideas can I consider?

Potential areas include spices, flour and grains, fruit and vegetable processing, bakery and snacks, oil and seed processing and other value-added foods. Explore our food processing business ideas in Gujarat.

What should I prepare before applying?

Prepare the proposed product, location, applicant details, existing or new unit status, machinery requirement, estimated project cost, financing plan and relevant documents for discussion with the applicable authorities and financing institution.
