ODOP: Tur based Products

Vadodara PMFME ODOP focus

The revised Ministry of Food Processing Industries Gujarat ODOP list identifies Tur based Products for Vadodara district. This can guide entrepreneurs evaluating dal and other value-added pulse-processing projects, subject to current PMFME conditions and technical feasibility.

Tur and pulse processing ideas to evaluate

  • Tur dal cleaning, grading and milling.
  • Packaged dal for retail and institutional markets.
  • Pulse flour and ingredient products.
  • Ready-to-cook pulse mixes where technically feasible.
  • Other value-added tur products based on market demand.

Project planning should consider raw-material quality, cleaning and milling losses, machinery capacity, packaging, food safety, utilities, working capital and market positioning.

Vadodara local context

Vadodara's agricultural and food-production context can support evaluation of pulse and other food-processing opportunities. The exact project should be matched to the proposed location, raw-material supply, utilities, workforce and market.

Project checklist

  1. Define the tur/pulse product and target market.
  2. Assess raw-material sourcing and expected processing capacity.
  3. Prepare machinery, premises, utilities and working-capital estimates.
  4. Check PMFME eligibility and admissible project costs.
  5. Prepare a practical project report and current quotations where required.
