ODOP: Coriander based products

Junagadh PMFME ODOP focus

The current Gujarat ODOP list identifies Coriander based products for Junagadh district. This makes coriander and suitable spice value addition an important direction to evaluate when developing an eligible PMFME project.

Coriander and spice-processing ideas

  • Cleaning, grading and hygienic packing of coriander.
  • Coriander powder with suitable grinding and sieving.
  • Blended spice products using coriander as an ingredient.
  • Retail-ready spice packs and other value-added formats with a clear market.

Good project planning should address raw-material quality, moisture control, grinding and packing equipment, food safety, shelf life, branding and distribution.

PMFME project preparation for Junagadh

  1. Define the product, capacity and customer segment.
  2. Estimate machinery, premises, utilities and working capital.
  3. Check current PMFME eligibility and admissible costs.
  4. Prepare a practical project report with current quotations where required.
  5. Confirm the proposed finance structure with the relevant bank.
