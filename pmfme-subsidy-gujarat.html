PMFME Subsidy in Gujarat

Understand the core subsidy framework before discussing project finance and eligible costs.

35% • Up to ₹10 lakh

For eligible individual micro food processing units, MoFPI states that credit-linked capital subsidy is available at 35% of eligible project cost, subject to a maximum of ₹10 lakh per unit.

Beneficiary contribution and bank finance

For the individual-unit component, the beneficiary contribution should be a minimum of 10%, with the balance required fund being a bank loan, subject to the applicable guidelines and bank appraisal.

What counts toward eligible project cost?

The PMFME guideline modification dated 18 May 2022 states that eligible project cost for individual micro food processing units comprises plant and machinery plus technical civil work. Land cost and rental or lease of a work shed are excluded. Technical civil work should not exceed 30% of the eligible project cost.

Subsidy is not simply 35% of every expense

The calculation is based on the eligible project cost, not automatically the total amount a promoter plans to spend. Each project should be checked against the applicable PMFME guidelines and the financing institution's appraisal.

Example

If an eligible project cost were ₹20 lakh, 35% would be ₹7 lakh. If the eligible project cost were ₹40 lakh, 35% would be ₹14 lakh, but the individual-unit ceiling would cap the subsidy at ₹10 lakh.

Official reference

For the detailed rules, refer to the MoFPI PMFME guideline modification dated 18 May 2022 and current official scheme guidance.
