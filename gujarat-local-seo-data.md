# Gujarat Local SEO Data Foundation

The local SEO build uses a source-controlled data layer rather than manually duplicated HTML.

## Current reference

The Gujarat Socio-Economic Review 2025-26 reports 34 districts and 267 talukas as the administrative setup as of 31 December 2025, while it separately reports 265 Taluka Panchayats. The site therefore records both figures and does not present them as interchangeable.

## 2025 restructuring

The 17 new talukas are recorded in `data/gujarat-local-seo-data.json`, with their parent/source talukas and districts.

## Local-page policy

- District pages require unique PMFME/ODOP/local food-processing information.
- Taluka pages require verified current administrative mapping.
- Village/area pages are created only when they add meaningful unique information.
- Avoid mass-generated doorway pages and duplicate keyword templates.
- Verify village transfers using the latest Gujarat Revenue Department Gazette.

## Source priority

1. Gujarat Government Gazette / Revenue Department.
2. Gujarat Directorate of Economics & Statistics.
3. District administration websites.
4. Secondary sources only for cross-checking.
