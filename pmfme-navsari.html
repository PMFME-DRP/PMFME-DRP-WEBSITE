PMFME Guidance for Navsari

Mango-focused food-processing guidance for micro-enterprise project planning.

Navsari ODOP focus

The revised Gujarat ODOP list identifies Mango based Products for Navsari district. Mango value addition can support several processing concepts when seasonality, sourcing, technology and market demand are workable.

Mango-processing ideas

  • Mango pulp and juice products.
  • Dehydrated mango and shelf-stable products.
  • Mango pickles and other processed formats.
  • Pulp-based beverages and value-added products.

Assess cold-chain or storage needs, food safety, packaging, shelf life and market positioning.

PMFME project checklist

  1. Define product and target market.
  2. Estimate machinery, premises, storage and working capital.
  3. Check current PMFME eligibility and admissible costs.
  4. Prepare a bankable project report and current quotations.
