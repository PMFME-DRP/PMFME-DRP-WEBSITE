PMFME Guidance for Aravalli

District-specific food-processing guidance built around Aravalli's Potato based Products ODOP opportunity.

Aravalli PMFME ODOP focus

The approved Gujarat ODOP list identifies Potato based Products for Aravalli district. Potato value addition can be evaluated as a starting point for a PMFME project, subject to current scheme conditions and business feasibility.

Potato processing ideas to evaluate

  • Potato chips and snack products.
  • Dehydrated potato, flakes or powder.
  • Potato flour and ready-to-cook mixes.
  • Other packaged value-added potato products.

Project selection should consider raw-material supply, seasonality, machinery, food safety, packaging, market demand and financial viability.

PMFME project checklist for Aravalli

  1. Define the product and target customer.
  2. Estimate machinery, premises, working capital and capacity.
  3. Check eligibility and admissible costs under current PMFME guidelines.
  4. Prepare a practical project report and current quotations where required.
  5. Discuss finance structure with the relevant bank before submission.
